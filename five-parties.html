Five parties, one keyword

See how the popup behaves when several parties reply to the same phrase. Hover, click or tap an underline. Only the short responses appear initially. Select a coloured sentence to expand its party name and longer response. Each response can be expanded or collapsed independently.

All five parties and their responses are invented for this interface test. No extension or external party repositories are needed.
