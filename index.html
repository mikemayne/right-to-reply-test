See Right to Reply in action

Right to Reply is a browser extension that gives participating political parties an opportunity to respond to specific keywords when they are mentioned in an article. It does not decide whether an article is accurate or misleading. It simply makes the party's approved response available to the reader.

This page simulates the experience without the extension. Hover over, click or tap any dotted underline to see a short response. Select the short statement to reveal the party name and longer response. All underlines and short statements use the same neutral styling. You can also use Tab and Enter.

All article content and replies are fictional placeholder text. The newspaper navigation below is decorative and does not lead anywhere.
